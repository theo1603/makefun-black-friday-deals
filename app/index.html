How these deals were checked

All prices checked 27 Nov 2026, 07:30 UTC.

Every deal on this page was read at its store or in a deals source that names the store, on Black Friday itself, 27 November 2026. The sale price and the usual price are shown side by side, and the discount is worked out from those two prices — never typed in by hand.

Each card carries a verdict: New low means the lowest price tracked for that product; Strong price means a genuinely good cut; Routine sale means the saving is small, the price returns often, or the reference price looks inflated. Two deals on this page are routine on purpose — a finder that calls everything a bargain is no use to anyone.
